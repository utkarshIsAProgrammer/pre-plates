# 12 — User Manual

**Project:** QuickCanteen | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-09-21
Audience: customers, teachers, canteen owners, admin. No technical knowledge assumed.

---

## 1. Getting Started (everyone)

1. Open the app URL (or scan the poster QR at the canteen entrance)
2. **Sign Up** → enter name, college email, password → account created
3. Log in any time with your email and password

> Your role decides what you see: **ordering app** for customers, **dashboard** for canteen owners, **admin panel** for administrators.

## 2. For Customers — Customers & Teachers

### 2.1 Browse & search
- Home shows every sub-canteen with **OPEN/CLOSED** status and average prep time
- Tap a canteen to see its full menu, or use the **search bar** to find an item across *all* canteens — you'll see who sells it and at what price
- Use **Veg only** and category filters to narrow down

### 2.2 Order (works across multiple canteens at once)
1. Tap **＋ Add** on items you want — from any canteen, they all go into one cart
2. Open the **cart** — items are grouped under each canteen with separate subtotals
3. Optionally add a note per item ("extra spicy", "no onion")
4. Tap **Place Order**
5. You get a **token number for each canteen** (e.g. `A-12` and `C-05`) and an estimated ready time

> 💳 **Payment:** pay **each canteen separately, cash or UPI, at its counter** when you collect.

### 2.3 Track & pick up
- Watch your order move: **Placed → Preparing → Ready → Picked Up** — live, no refresh
- When an order turns **Ready** you get a notification; tap it to see the giant token
- Walk to the counter, show/say your token (e.g. "A-12"), pay, collect. No queue. ✅

### 2.4 Cancelling & reordering
- Cancel is available **only until the owner accepts** the order
- **Reorder** any past order from Order History — items go straight back into your cart

### 2.5 Notifications
- The 🔔 icon shows an unread count; tap for all updates
- You are notified when your order is accepted, rejected (with reason), preparing, or ready

## 3. For Canteen Owners

### 3.1 Receiving orders
- Your **Live Orders** screen refreshes by itself — a **sound** plays when a new order arrives
- Each card shows token, items, notes, customer name/department, and amount
- You see **only orders meant for your canteen**

### 3.2 Processing an order
1. **Accept** (or **Reject** with a reason — sold out / closing / other)
2. Tap **Preparing** when you start cooking
3. Tap **Ready** when done — the customer is notified instantly and shown their token
4. When the customer arrives and says the token, confirm it, take payment, tap **Paid & Picked Up**

### 3.3 Managing your menu
- **Menu** tab → **Add item**: name, price, category, veg/non-veg, photo, prep time
- **Sold out?** Flip the availability switch — the item can't be ordered immediately but stays visible
- Edit or delete items any time

### 3.4 Day summary
- See today's order count, revenue, and how many orders are in each status

## 4. For Admin

### 4.1 Set up a sub-canteen
1. **Canteens → Add Canteen**
2. Fill: name, **token prefix** (`A`, `JU`…), hall location, open/close times
3. **Assign an owner** — that user gets dashboard access to this canteen only
4. The canteen appears on the customer home page instantly

### 4.2 Manage users
- Change anyone's role (customer ↔ owner ↔ admin) or deactivate an account

### 4.3 Open/close control
- Toggle any canteen closed — it disappears from browsing and stops taking orders

## 5. FAQ

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Do I pay online? | No — pay each canteen at its counter (cash/UPI) when collecting. |
| Can I order from two canteens at once? | Yes — one cart, separate tokens and separate payments per canteen. |
| What if I miss the "Ready" notification? | Open **My Orders** — Ready orders show a pulsing token. |
| The owner rejected my order. Why? | The reason is shown in the notification (usually sold out). Nothing was owed. |
| My token was called but I'm not there? | Food is kept aside briefly; the owner will try the next tokens. Collect ASAP. |
| Does the canteen still take walk-up orders? | That's the canteen's choice — the app is a pre-order lane, not a replacement. |

## 6. Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "Reconnecting…" banner | Wait a few seconds — the app restores itself. If stuck, refresh. |
| Order stuck on "Placed" > 5 min | Call the canteen counter directly; they may not have seen it. |
| Can't add item to cart | It's marked **Sold out** — check the canteen's menu for alternatives. |
| Didn't get login OTP / email | v1 has no email verification — just log in. |
| Cart empty after reopening browser | Cart lives in your browser storage; clearing site data clears it. Re-adding takes 2 taps. |
